# How to Use AI to Solve and Understand Tough Exam Questions

## Introduction
Using AI to instantly copy and paste answers for your homework won't help you clear your exams. If you do not understand the underlying concept, you will struggle when a similar question appears in a test. 

This guide teaches you how to turn an AI tool into a personal tutor that breaks down complex math, physics, or chemistry problems step-by-step.

## Prerequisites
To follow this guide, you need a free account on an AI platform like ChatGPT or Claude.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Avoid the "Just Give Me the Answer" Prompt
If you only ask the AI for the final answer, it will skip the intermediate logical steps. This leaves you unprepared for variations of that problem on exam day.

### Step 2: Use the "Teacher Prompt" for Better Explanations
Standard prompts usually give you a flat answer without showing the underlying logic. To turn the AI into a patient tutor, assign it a role before pasting your question. 

Use this specific prompt structure to get a detailed breakdown:

```
Act as an experienced physics and math teacher. Explain the core concept 
behind the following question, and then guide me through the solution 
step-by-step: [Paste your question here]
```
### Step 3: Verify the Logic
Once the AI provides the solution, verify the steps manually against your textbook or class notes. If a specific transition confuses you, ask the AI: "Why did you use that specific formula in step 3?"


## Conclusion
Write precise prompts to get high-quality explanations. When you clearly define 
the AI's role, you receive a structured response that makes complex exam 
concepts much faster to grasp.
